# A design team made of agents

**Alt text:** A brief goes into a team of 10 specialist AI agents (strategist, design lead, writer, builder, accessibility, critic and more) that share one design state document and hand work to each other. The result goes to "you", the creative director. Two modes: Direct pauses at every handoff; Auto runs on its own and stops when reviewers disagree.

**One big idea:** Agents check the work. Taste stays with the designer.

- **What:** Designpowers, an open-source (MIT) design workflow of 10 specialist agents that move a brief from discovery to shipping, writing to one shared design state and pushing back on each other.
- **Who:** MC Dean (Marie-Claire Dean), designer; writes *MC Dean percolates* on Substack.
- **Agent check:** Partly autonomous. The 13 phases are a fixed order, but in Auto mode the agents run the pipeline themselves and decide when to stop and ask (when an agent recommends rethinking or reviewers disagree). In Direct mode it pauses at every handoff.
- **Why it matters:** She splits the job clearly: agents verify correctness, accessibility and fit to the brief; judging whether it is beautiful stays human.
