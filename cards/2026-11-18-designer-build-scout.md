# Designers' first agents are mostly words

**One big idea:** Designers building agents today mostly *write*: rules, critique methods and roles in plain-language files. The agent does the rest.

- **What:** A personal design assistant made of markdown files; five small "auditor" agents, each with one job (accessibility, design system, performance, QA).
- **Who:** Dianne Alter (Head of Design, The Design Project) and Stefan Imhoff (design engineer).
- **Why it matters:** Writing intent and judgement is a design skill. And almost nobody documents these builds visually yet.
